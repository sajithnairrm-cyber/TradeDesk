Add to Home Screen
iPhone and iPad install from the Share menu.
1Tap the Share button in Safari’s toolbar — the square with an arrow pointing up.
2Scroll down and choose Add to Home Screen.
3Tap Add. Trade Desk appears on your home screen and opens full screen from then on.

This only works in Safari. Chrome and Firefox on iOS cannot install web apps — an iOS restriction, not a fault in the desk.
